# Battlecard (grounded asset generation)

**Status:** designed · **Needs:** company-setup, this-is-that, ideal-customer-profile, fact-freshness, brand-guide

## Inputs

- **Our product and the competitor's product,** named explicitly rather than guessed
- **Audience:** e.g. CTO, CFO, engineering manager. The same facts are framed differently for each: cost and ROI
  for a CFO, architecture and integration for a CTO.
- **Asset type:** battlecard, one-pager, comparison table, talking points, objection handling. Each has its own
  structure and length.
- **Example assets:** past assets of that type, so the draft follows an established format and voice

## Battlecard sections

1. **Quick take:** where we win, in one line
2. **Who it's for:** the overlap of the two ICPs, and the deals where each is strongest
3. **This is that / this isn't that:** what's equivalent, and the real differences (from this-is-that)
4. **Why we win**, with proof: customer outcomes and numbers, with sources
5. **Where they win:** honest, so sellers aren't blindsided
6. **Objections and answers:** what their sellers and their leaders say (from person-perspective), and the response
7. **Landmines to set:** questions that expose their weak spots
8. **Pricing comparison:** plans side by side, with dates
9. **Recent moves:** their last launches, acquisitions and sunsets (from the timeline)

## How it works

1. **Retrieve** the structured facts for both products, filtered by audience relevance.
2. **Draft** against the example assets, so the format stays fixed and only the content is written.
3. **Check freshness:** hand the exact list of facts used (claim, source, date) to fact-freshness, which
   re-checks anything past its staleness window. Pricing and features age fast; founding dates don't.
4. **Flag** any claim that couldn't be verified, instead of shipping it silently.
5. **Lay it out** in the company's brand (brand-guide).

## Output

The asset, plus a source list. Every factual claim links to its source and carries its last-confirmed date.
