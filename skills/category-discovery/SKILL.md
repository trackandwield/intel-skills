# Category discovery

**Status:** designed · **Needs:** landscape-taxonomy

## How it works

Look for clusters of content from several independent sources about something that maps to no existing category
(e.g. a wave of posts about a new agent-orchestration pattern). A single mention is noise; independent repetition
over time is signal.

## Output

A proposed category with its evidence (which sources, how many, over what period), reviewed before it joins the
taxonomy.
