# Daily findings report

**Status:** designed · **Needs:** extraction, this-is-that, company-timeline

## Sections

- Products, companies and people seen for the first time
- Notable changes to known ones: funding, launches, renames, sunsets, role changes, pricing changes
- New or changed likeness groups, and borderline pairs to review (this-is-that)
- Direction calls that shifted (release-note-trajectory)
- The day's strongest articles, episodes and videos, each with a one-line why and a link
- Proposed sources and people waiting for approval

Drawn in the tracked company's brand. Companion to the errors-and-flags report (run-report), which covers what
went wrong rather than what was found.
