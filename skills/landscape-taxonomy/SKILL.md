# Landscape taxonomy

**Status:** designed · **Used by:** this-is-that, category-discovery, multi-category-classifier

## How it works

When a new term appears (a technique, an architecture, a product category), it's checked against the map. A clean
match is tagged there. Anything that doesn't fit is flagged as a candidate new category or subcategory for review.

## Output

A versioned taxonomy: categories, subcategories, their associated terms, the date each was first seen, and
example sources. It's the map everything else checks against.
