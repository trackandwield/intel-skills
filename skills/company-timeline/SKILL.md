# Company timeline

**Status:** designed · **Needs:** company-setup (events, people), release-note-trajectory, acquisition-integration, product-sunset

## Lanes

- **Company:** founding, funding rounds, leadership changes, size milestones
- **Products:** one bar per product from launch to today or to its end, with renames and major releases marked,
  and acquired products shown joining
- **Acquisitions and sunsets:** dated markers linked to their analyses
- **People:** founders and key people, with when each joined, changed role and left
- **Direction:** trajectory and sunset calls, dated, so it's visible when the picture shifted

## Behaviour

- Built from stored records, never typed by hand, and regenerated after each run.
- Every marker links to its source.
- Competitors can share the same time axis, to compare paths.
- Drawn in the company's brand (brand-guide).
