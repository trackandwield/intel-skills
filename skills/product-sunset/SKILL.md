# Product sunset analysis

**Status:** designed · **Needs:** release-note-trajectory, company-timeline

## How it works

1. **Detect:**
   - deprecation and end-of-life notices in release notes
   - "legacy" labels
   - sunset and migration pages
   - products disappearing from pricing or navigation
   - product pages redirecting
   - docs removed, confirmed against archived copies
2. **Context:**
   - the replacement, and the migration path offered
   - who is affected
   - how long the product lived, and how actively it was developed before the end
   - whether it came from an acquisition
3. **Interpretation,** with evidence and confidence:
   - narrowing focus
   - moving upmarket or downmarket
   - consolidating after an acquisition
   - ceding the space to a competitor
   - cutting costs

   Each reading is checked against the company's other signals and against competitors' moves in the same space.

## Output

A sunset record (product, announced date, end date, replacement, reason given, likely real reason), a marker on
the company timeline, and an entry in the daily findings report.
